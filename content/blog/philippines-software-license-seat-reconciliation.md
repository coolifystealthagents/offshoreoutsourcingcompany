# Software License Seat Reconciliation for a Philippines Operations Team

*October 11, 2026*

Compare assigned, active, billed, and approved software seats without transferring purchasing or access authority. The operating goal is a reviewable administrative lane: approved sources enter, routine checks happen under written rules, exceptions remain visible, and the person with authority makes the decision.

## Define four populations before counting anything

A useful reconciliation keeps purchased entitlements, identity assignments, measured activity, and business approvals separate. They answer different questions. A billed seat may be unassigned; an assigned account may be dormant; a shared integration may show no human login; an active user may have no documented owner approval. Freeze extracts from the contract register, vendor console, identity provider, and approved roster at declared times. Keep the original identifiers and extraction timestamps. The Philippines analyst matches records under written rules but does not decide that inactivity alone permits access removal or contract cancellation.

## Use an application-specific identity key

Email address is convenient but weak when aliases, renamed users, guests, service accounts, and acquisitions are involved. Establish a match hierarchy for each application: immutable directory identifier where supported, approved employee or contractor ID, then reviewed email aliases. Record one-to-many and many-to-one matches as exceptions. Never merge records only to make totals agree. Keep service accounts, test users, break-glass accounts, bots, and external collaborators in explicit categories, because treating them as employees can create false savings and unsafe deprovisioning recommendations.

## Reconcile price logic without approving spend

Capture product tier, billing interval, currency, committed minimum, overage rule, renewal window, and owner-approved cost allocation from the controlling commercial record. The analyst may reproduce arithmetic and flag discrepancies between invoices and console counts. Procurement and finance decide whether the vendor interpretation, contract term, tax treatment, credit, or renewal action is correct. Preserve the invoice line and contract pointer beside each calculation. A spreadsheet estimate is working evidence, not authorization to buy, downgrade, dispute, or accrue an amount.

## Create exception reasons that lead somewhere

Useful reason codes include unapproved assignment, departed person, role changed, inactive but owner review required, duplicate identity, unknown account, service identity, license tier mismatch, invoice quantity mismatch, and inventory gap. Each code needs a receiving owner and response clock. Avoid a single “unused” bucket: it invites staff to remove access without context. The queue should show the observed facts, the evidence source, the requested decision, and the next safe action. A coordinator can prepare a removal ticket only after the named owner records approval.

## Protect the extracts

License inventories reveal names, roles, applications, privileged access, and sometimes usage behavior. Limit columns to what the reconciliation needs, store extracts in an approved restricted location, and set a deletion schedule. Use named accounts and multifactor authentication. CISA’s performance goals and NIST’s Cybersecurity Framework provide current control context, but the company’s security and privacy owners determine applicable safeguards. Do not email full rosters to convenience recipients or paste them into unsupported automation. Redact personal data from aggregate reporting when the reviewer only needs counts and causes.

## Work a realistic mismatch

An annual design-tool invoice bills 84 seats. The console shows 82 assigned identities, including three guests and two accounts with renamed email domains. Activity data shows nine accounts with no event in 90 days. The analyst matches immutable IDs, isolates the guests, links the renamed accounts, and reports the remaining gaps. Department owners review business need; security reviews access; procurement reviews commitment terms. The analyst does not recommend deleting all nine inactive accounts or claim that two unassigned seats automatically produce a refund.

## Close with evidence on both sides

Report counts by entitlement, assignment, activity state, approval state, and resolved exception. Show unmatched records separately and retain denominators. Sample from invoices forward to assignments and from active accounts backward to approval. Record the export time because vendor consoles change continuously. Reconciliation quality means a reviewer can explain each material difference and see the owner’s decision. Savings may follow, but they are not the quality metric. Re-run after hiring changes, acquisitions, contract amendments, identity migrations, and vendor billing changes rather than treating one clean month as permanent control.
